How EMI Is Estimated

Standard reducing-balance formula: EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n − 1), where P is principal loan amount, r is monthly interest rate (annualRate / 12 / 100), and n is total monthly instalments (tenureYears × 12). If the interest rate is set to 0%, monthly instalment equals P / n. Actual lender terms, floating rate resets, credit eligibility, and statutory charges vary.
